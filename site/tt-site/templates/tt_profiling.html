Hardware counters

A second container (1 warmup, 3 samples per accepted testcase) reads the Tensix performance counters of every core after each kernel: FPU and SFPU busy cycles, unpacker and packer busy cycles, and for each of the three instruction threads (unpack, math, pack) the stalled cycles, instructions issued and the stall reasons (waiting on a semaphore, on SrcA/SrcB, or on another unit to go idle). The record page shows each unit's busy fraction of the kernel window averaged over cores, the per-thread stall breakdown, and all 85 raw counters with a CSV download. Use them to explain where a kernel's time goes; they do not change the ranked device time.
